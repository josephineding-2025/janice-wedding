我要全心赞美 My Heart Will Praise You, Lord
Verse
全心感谢 全心赞美 进入祢的院 With all my heart, I will give thanks And enter Your court
全心颂赞 献上为祭 是嘴唇的果子 Offering my praise to You, Lord, It’s the fruit of my lips
在祢里面 一无挂虑 喜乐满溢 In Your presence, free of worries, Joy overflows
常常感谢 不住祷告 盼望在于祢 Always grateful, always praying, My hope is in You!
Chorus
我要全心赞美
My heart will praise You, Lord
跳舞颂扬祢名
I’ll dance before Your throne
在祢殿中一日 胜过在世上千日
One day with You, Is better than a thousand elsewhere
我要全心赞美 赞美还要赞美不停
My heart will praise You, Lord, I’ll never stop praising Your name
义人棚里欢呼声永不息
The righteous will declare Your victory!
